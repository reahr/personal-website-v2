A new brand and website for a holiday gift-wrapping business growing into year-round gifting

From seasonal pop-up to gifting destination

A Queens and Long Island gift-wrapping business expanding into curated and corporate gifting.

The goal

A brand and website that explain schedules, packages and gifts, and turn interest into bookings.

My role

Research, branding, UX and UI design, plus front-end polish.

Timeline

Holiday seasons 2023 and 2024

Worked with

Discovery

What the site had to do

  • Show where and when to find them across three locations
  • Make wrapping prices and in-home packages easy to compare
  • Showcase curated gift picks
  • Capture corporate leads for a new gifting program

Brand refresh

Black, gold and a bow: a premium gifting identity

The business was growing from seasonal mall pop-ups into curated and corporate gifting, so the brand had to feel premium all year, not only at the holidays.

Why refresh

The existing branding was built for holiday pop-ups. Corporate and year-round gifting needed an identity that signals quality before anyone reads a word.

What changed

I updated the logo and brand assets to match the client's vision: black and gold with a red bow, a mark that reads as a wrapped gift.

Where it shows up

Gold tints, a gray scale and Inter carry the brand across web, print and social, from the home hero to the Golden Gift Program.

Key flows

Three jobs the new pages had to do

Schedules: when and where to get wrapped

Three locations with hours. Holiday drop-off dates. In-home booking: package, date, confirmation, add to calendar.

Shopping lists: curated gifts, easy to browse

Gift listings with sorting. Quick view and item pages. “You may also like” picks.

Interest flows: turning interest into leads

Golden Gift Program for corporate orders. Quote form with validation and a success state. Newsletter signup.

Design to build

Making handoff smooth, then checking the build

A component kit

Buttons, icon buttons and a date picker built on Material 3, with every state documented.

A type and contrast spec

An Inter type scale for desktop and mobile, checked against WCAG AA and AAA contrast.

Responsive layouts

Desktop, tablet and mobile versions of key pages.

Hands-on front end
