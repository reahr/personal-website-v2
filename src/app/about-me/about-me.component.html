About

/ree•uh/
I’m a product manager based in New York City who ships products end to end — from first user research to the metrics that tell me it worked.

I started as a front-end engineer before moving into product and UX at RelPro, a B2B sales intelligence platform. There, I turned customer calls and live user sessions into roadmap decisions, and shipped features that made a real, measurable difference in how people used the product.

My background in both design and engineering lets me work the whole lifecycle — guiding designers, running research, and sitting with engineers on technical feasibility. I care most about shipping things that are genuinely useful, accessible by default, and backed by a clear story in the data, not just a launch date.

As for fun, I love boxing (to relieve some stress when curveballs are added to the roadmap), reading (to distract myself from those curveballs), and traveling (to avoid them altogether... jk) — with a shared doc, a spreadsheet and a color-coded itinerary for every trip. That habit is actually what led me to build Sillage, a trip planner I tested on a real four-day trip to Medellín and Cartagena. I also love watching action movies and psychological thrillers.
